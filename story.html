Happy Crumbs started from a small kitchen and a big dream: to share warm, soft, and happy bread with everyone.

From A Small Kitchen

It all began with my hobby cooking and baking bread which started when I would bake for my family and relatives. The aroma of fresh bread would fill our modest home.

My family loved it so much that they encouraged me to bake even more. Starting with just one small oven, I dream of growing into a cozy bakery that everyone knows.

I haven't opened a bakery yet, but this is what I envision my shop might look like once I finally realize that dream.
